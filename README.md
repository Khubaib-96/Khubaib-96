## 🧠 About Me

I am a **Senior AI Engineer at [QLU.ai](https://qlu.ai/)**, building production LLM systems across enterprise search, document intelligence, people and company discovery, outreach automation, and context-aware conversational AI.

My work focuses on connecting LLMs with structured data, retrieval systems, real-time pipelines, and reliable reasoning workflows that can operate over complex business information.

* 🔍 Building **Hybrid RAG and semantic search systems** using dense, lexical, and structured retrieval
* 🤖 Developing **context-aware AI agents** with tool calling, multi-step reasoning, and conversational search
* 🗂️ Engineering ingestion pipelines for **emails, calendars, meetings, transcripts, LinkedIn threads, and PDFs**
* ⚡ Designing low-latency AI services using **FastAPI, asynchronous processing, caching, fallbacks, and SSE**
* 📄 Conducting applied AI and NLP research through **SemEval, CLEF, IEEE, and ICNLP**
* 🎓 Computer Science graduate from **Habib University**, with a **3.82/4.00 CGPA**
